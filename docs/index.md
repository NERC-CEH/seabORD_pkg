## Overview

**SeabORD-R** is an R package for predicting the demographic impacts of
displacement and barrier effects from offshore renewable developments
(ORDs) on seabird populations.

The model simulates how changes in foraging behaviour and movement
patterns affect the birds’ survival and productivity. These demographic
responses are estimated through species-specific time and energy
budgets.

Potential impacts of offshore renewable developments are assessed by
comparing a baseline scenario with one or more development scenarios
containing offshore renewable infrastructure.
